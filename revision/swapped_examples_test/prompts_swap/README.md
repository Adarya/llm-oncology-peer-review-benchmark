# Swapped example journals

`06_chief_reviewer_swapped_examples.md` is `prompts/06_chief_reviewer.md` with one change: in the tier-examples table, the 14 study journals are replaced by 13 oncology journals that are not in the study and are in the same impact-factor band. The four examples that are not study journals (Lancet Oncology, European J Cancer, BMC Cancer, World J Clin Oncol) are unchanged.

Replacement journals, with bands read from Journal Citation Reports on 30 October 2026:

- Tier 1 (JIF > 20): Annals of Oncology, Cancer Cell, Cancer Discovery, Molecular Cancer.
- Tier 2 (JIF 10 to 20): J Exp Clin Cancer Res, J Immunother Cancer, JNCI, Neuro-Oncology, Leukemia.
- Tier 3 (JIF 4 to 9.9): British J Cancer, International J Cancer.
- Tier 4 (JIF < 4): Cancer Medicine, J Cancer Res Clin Oncol. Tier 4 lists three examples instead of four.
