# Data Notice

This repository does not redistribute source PubMed Central full-text article
XML files or blinded manuscript markdown files.

The benchmark corpus was derived from the PubMed Central Open Access Subset.
PMC states that Open Access Subset articles are made available under Creative
Commons or similar license terms, but license terms vary by article and users
are responsible for complying with each article's copyright and license terms.

For that reason, this public repository keeps:

- analysis code;
- prompt and schema files;
- model-generated review outputs;
- derived analysis tables;
- figures;
- PMCID-level analytical sample metadata.

It does not keep:

- raw JATS/XML article files;
- full-text blinded manuscripts;
- manuscript drafts for the paper.

To reconstruct full-text inputs, retrieve articles directly from PMC using the
official PMC Open Access Subset retrieval services and verify the license terms
for each article before redistribution or reuse.

Official references:

- PMC Open Access Subset: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/tools/openftlist/
- PMC Copyright Notice: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/about/copyright/
